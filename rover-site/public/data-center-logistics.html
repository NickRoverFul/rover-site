The Problem

Distance is
downtime.

Northern Virginia is the largest data center market in the world, and the operators there run on tight replacement windows. When a drive, switch, optic, or power supply fails, the clock starts immediately.

If your inventory sits in a warehouse three states away, your customer waits on transit time you can't compress — no matter how fast your team processes the order. Meanwhile the distributors who can put a part in someone's hands the same day win the account, and keep it.

The gap: plenty of hardware distributors sell into this corridor. Far fewer have inventory physically near it. Warehousing locally is the cheapest way to close that gap — you don't need a facility, a lease, or staff to do it.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you stock parts yourselves?

No — and that's deliberate. We don't buy, own, or resell hardware. We warehouse and fulfill your inventory, so you keep full control of what you carry, what it costs, and what you charge for it.

Is there a minimum volume?

Forward stocking is quoted per account on space and dispatch activity rather than a fixed order minimum — a spares pool might sit still for weeks and then move urgently. Our 250 orders/month minimum applies to D2C e-commerce fulfillment, not to this. Tell us what you'd stage and we'll price it.

How fast can an order go out?

Our standard turnaround is within 48 hours of receiving an order. If you need tighter timelines for downtime-critical parts, tell us up front — that's a conversation worth having before you send inventory, not after.

What does it cost?

Storage is billed monthly by pallet or box, and fulfillment is billed per order. Because hardware varies so much in size and weight, we quote this individually rather than posting a flat rate. Send us your details and we'll price it.

Are you a licensed, insured facility?

Rover Fulfillment LLC is a registered Virginia business operating a commercial warehouse in Chantilly. If your account requires specific insurance coverage, certifications, or chain-of-custody documentation, raise it early and we'll tell you plainly what we can and can't meet today.
