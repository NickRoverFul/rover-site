FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers.

Is there a minimum shipment size?

No minimums — ever. We work with brands at every stage, whether you're sending 10 units or 10,000.

How fast do you process orders?

We process and ship orders within 48 hours of receipt. During peak periods we'll communicate any changes proactively.

Do you handle hazmat or oversized items?

Contact us with the specifics of your product and we'll let you know. We handle a wide range of product types and can accommodate many special requirements.

Do you provide photos of prepped items?

Yes. We can provide photos of prepped inventory upon request. Your client portal also gives you visibility into your inventory status at all times.

Do you handle returns?

Yes. We offer returns processing and inspection. Returned items are checked, logged, and restocked or flagged based on your preferences.

How does the one-time integration setup work?

We connect our order management software to your store (Shopify, Amazon, Walmart, etc.). There is a one-time setup fee for this — after that, orders sync automatically at no additional cost.
